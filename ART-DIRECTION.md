# Three Stars, One World

## Reference language

Inspected all eight supplied Rumi/Mira/Zoey images, the reference checklist, approved character selection and hub captures. No environment sheets or official emblem are supplied. The plaza motif is an original three-petal star, not an official HUNTR/X logo.

- **World:** luminous twilight, ink-blue architecture, plum paving, warm porcelain edges. Gold outlines make destinations readable. Avoid black shadows and saturated neon everywhere.
- **Rumi:** amethyst braid, sunflower jacket, pink waist accent. Braided curves, elongated pendant and violet/gold light.
- **Mira:** wine-magenta twin tails, black top, yellow skirt, chain/pendant. Paired sweeping ribbons and raspberry/gold light.
- **Zoey:** dark buns/fringe, teal top, gold accents. Paired round forms, teal fans and turquoise/gold light.
- **Magic:** warm star cores with violet, pink and teal satellite petals. Cloud curls and hanging tassels echo the costume embroidery and ornaments.
- **Concert:** three portrait banners, layered open gold frame, softly pulsing character-colored footlights. Golden wakes a brief gold halo; no strobe or new lights.
- **Garden:** deep jade leaves, pearl-pink blossoms, open cloud arch. Petals lift as a girl approaches.
- **Tea / bubbles:** giant readable tea cup with pearls; round iridescent bubbles and friendly face. Keep their existing play entrances open.
- **Materials:** satin painted surfaces, warm metal trim, soft luminous inlays. Baked canvas art and merged opaque scenery; a few instanced accents animate in the existing loop.

## Composition and motion

Entry frames the selected girl, an airy floating star over the walkable plaza, the framed concert and a layered distant city. Keep the center and paths clear. Large activity silhouettes provide recognition before lettering. Small charms, blooming flowers and rising bubbles reward proximity without becoming collectibles. Character accents affect a few lights and ornaments only. Motion is slow and continuous; MAGIC briefly opens the centerpiece. Preserve reduced motion and pause behavior.
